Mid-term review · Oct 2026

Current flow · 1. Inconvenient

Too many steps for customers and cashiers

Customers

  • Open the app, log in, sometimes wait for an OTP
  • Find a voucher that works at this store
  • Read the terms, branches and expiry themselves
  • Read the code aloud in a crowd, or hand over an unlocked phone

POS staff

  • Listen to or look at the code on the customer’s phone
  • Type 10–16 characters by hand, mixing up 0/O and 1/I
  • Barcodes fail to scan on glaring or cracked screens
  • Every brand has a different flow

8 steps from opening the app to the voucher being marked as used, while the customer waits at the counter.

Source: Item 1.1 – Current in-store voucher flow · 02

Current flow · 2. Weak security

Voucher codes leak easily and can be reused

Codes are shown in readable form

Screenshots, people behind in the queue and store cameras can all capture the code.

No time limit, not tied to anyone

Whoever has the code can spend it, at any branch, at any time.

Codes are locked late

At non-integrated merchants, a code is locked minutes later, or only at reconciliation. Until then it can be reused.

Cashiers see the full code

They can copy it for later. Records stop at store level, so the operator cannot be traced.

Source: Item 1.1 – Current in-store voucher flow · 03

Competitors

No voucher platform offers in-store QR scanning yet

DIRECT COMPETITORS

E-voucher platforms

  • Surveyed: [voucher platform names]
  • Still use static codes or barcodes: the customer shows the code, the cashier types or scans it
  • None lets customers scan a QR at the counter to redeem

DIFFERENT MODEL

E-wallets and banks

  • MoMo, ZaloPay, VietQR use QR, but as payment gateways: they move the customer’s own money
  • Their offers are discounts attached to a payment, unlike prepaid vouchers bought by companies as gifts
  • Useful only as a reference for QR habits

Got It can be first, and must design the flow so it does not look like a payment gateway.

Source: Item 1.2 – Market research (Mai Hiên) · 05

Solution

Reverse the scan: the code never leaves the app

Today · 8 steps

  1. Open the app, log in
  2. Find the voucher in the gift wallet
  3. Read terms, branches, expiry
  4. Tap “Use” to show the code
  5. Read the code aloud to the cashier
  6. Cashier types 10–16 characters
  7. POS validates and deducts
  8. Confirm as used

Tier 0.5 · 5 steps

  1. Open the app, scan the QR sticker at the counter
  2. App filters vouchers valid at this store
  3. Pick a voucher; app creates a one-time code valid for 2–3 minutes
  4. Cashier scans the code on screen
  5. POS validates and confirms — same as today

The read-aloud step is gone — the step that causes leaks and reuse. Everything that touches the merchant’s POS stays the same.

Source: Item 2.1 and discussion with the Merchant team · 06

Solution

Four integration tiers, brands choose their level

TIER 0

No integration

Brand does: nothing

Cost: zero

Fixes: code leaks, reuse, typing errors

TIER 0.5 · PROPOSED

QR sticker at the counter

Brand does: put up a QR printed by Got It

Cost: close to zero

Also fixes: customers bringing vouchers for the wrong store

TIER 1

Real-time results

Brand does: receive results via API or portal

Cost: a few person-days

Also fixes: balance mismatch on partial use

TIER 2

Deep POS integration

Brand does: a small project on their side

Cost: real, only if the brand wants it

Also fixes: internal fraud at the counter

Start at tier 0.5, move to tier 1 through POS vendors per the roadmap · 07

Rollout

Integrate once with a POS vendor, reach hundreds of brands.

Small and mid-size brands do not build their own POS — they use KiotViet, Sapo, iPOS, Haravan, Odoo. Got It brings a feature to the vendor instead of asking each brand.

Integration process with POS vendors

01 Pick priority vendors

Rank KiotViet, Sapo, iPOS, Haravan, Odoo by Got It sales through them and number of brands using them.

MC, Data/BI

02 Sign a partnership

Got It becomes a feature in the vendor’s ecosystem. Agree on fees, service levels and data security.

MC, Legal

03 Open API and sandbox

Got It provides APIs to validate codes, generate per-store QRs and push real-time results, plus a test environment.

Got It Engineering

04 Vendor builds a plugin

Add a “Got It voucher” button to the checkout screen. Got It engineers support them directly throughout.

Vendor, Got It Engineering

05 Test and pilot

Check security, offline cases, cancellations and refunds. Pilot with 1–2 brands on that vendor.

PD, Operations

06 Switch on for more brands

Brands just turn the feature on in their POS settings, no code. MC activates them from a list.

MC, Vendor

Brands with their own POS (tier 2) connect directly via API, sharing steps 03–05 · 09

Winning merchants over

What merchants gain by upgrading

No more losses from reused codes

Codes are locked at the counter; only the first validation is accepted. Fake vouchers do not get through.

Faster checkout

One scan instead of typing 10–16 characters. Fewer errors, fewer arguments at peak hours.

Get paid sooner

Automatic reconciliation from real-time results. Payout cycle cut from [T+__] to [T+1].

More customers from Got It

Priority placement in the app and in corporate gifting campaigns.

Store-level data

Redemptions, peak hours and bill size per branch, available right away.

Accurate partial use

The counter sends the exact amount used. No balance mismatches, no complaints to handle later.

Commission stays the same: merchants get more value, with no reason to renegotiate · 10

Value for Got It

What Got It gains

Control over every transaction

Know where, when and for how much each code was used. Less fraud, fewer support tickets.

Easier corporate sales

Secure vouchers with proof of use. First mover while no voucher platform does this.

Faster network growth

Each POS vendor opens up hundreds of brands. Adding a brand costs almost nothing.

Merchant retention

Being built into the POS creates a lasting tie that competitors find hard to replace.

Base for new products

Factory Rewards, QR scan campaigns, vouchers auto-applied from the wallet.

In return

Got It covers most of the switching cost, but does not cut commission.

Pushback

Three concerns from the Merchant team

MC-1 · COST

“Brands won’t accept another integration because it costs them — and Got It is the one who needs them.”

Tiers 0 and 0.5: brands write no code, pay nothing, change nothing at the counter.

MC-2 · COMMISSION

“It will look like a payment gateway, and brands will push for lower commission.”

No contract changes. No “payment” wording in the app, no amount field. But design alone cannot fully solve this — we need a number.

MC-3 · EASE OF USE

“How do we make it convenient and generate the best voucher code?”

Convenience comes from removing read-aloud and typing 10–16 characters — not from how the code is generated.

Source: discussion with the Merchant team, Sep 15, 2026 · 14
